# 22 November 2026 — The World in One Day

One image that sums up Sunday, 22 November 2026's world news, built for the
make.fun bounty [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122).

## What happened that day

Five stories, each verified against live reporting from 22 November 2026:

1. **COP31 collapses with no deal** — Antalya, Türkiye. After two weeks and two
   days of overtime, the UN climate talks end without a pact: the EU and others
   reject a text that never names fossil fuels. UN chief Guterres calls the
   outcome "deeply disappointing". The longest COP on record.
2. **Drone hits Kyiv flats; 14 missing** — A Russian drone partly destroys an
   apartment block near Kyiv. Rescuers search the rubble for at least 14 people.
3. **Minister quits over AI exam cheating** — Seoul. South Korea's education
   minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns days before the college entrance exams, after
   a cheating scandal.
4. **Factory fire kills 3; 36 missing** — Banten, Indonesia. A blaze at a candy
   factory in Cikande kills three workers; dozens are still missing as rescuers
   search the ruins.
5. **Verstappen wins Las Vegas GP** — Max Verstappen takes his third Las Vegas
   win in four years, beating Norris by 0.639s in a 35-lead-change thriller.

The image is a newspaper front page: the date is the masthead, the COP31
collapse leads, and the other four stories fill a two-by-two grid, each with
its own picture. Every story is real and from 22 November 2026 — nothing is
invented, and nothing from an earlier day is shown as that day's news.

## How it works

`entry.png` is the image (1024×1024, PNG). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only) copies it to the output directory the grader provides:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

A straight copy, so two runs always write identical bytes.
